## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a forward-looking, high-energy fashion narrative that bridges editorial polish with accessible, multichannel retail agility across Women, Men, and Kids. 

The aesthetic is built on high-chroma vibrancy over luminous, pristine surfaces. It fuses sleek modern minimalism with kinetic, fashion-forward visual cues: vivid ambient glows, sculpted soft corners, and snappy microinteractions that impart premium quality without clutter. 

The emotional tone evokes curiosity, self-expression, and confidence. Interfaces are fast, decisive, and rhythmically responsive, catering simultaneously to trend-conscious shoppers on mobile devices and store managers operating dense inventory matrices.

## Colors

The color architecture leverages pure contrast to maintain retail clarity while preserving electric brand recognition.

- **Primary (`#FF007A`)**: The hero accent. Used for primary conversion triggers, active category highlights, priority promotions, and energetic hero badges.
- **Secondary (`#4F46E5`)**: Electric Cobalt. Balances the high intensity of magenta, delivering structure to navigation chrome, secondary calls to action, AI chat agent triggers, and omnichannel touchpoints.
- **Tertiary (`#FF6B00`)**: Neon Tangerine. Used purposefully for flash sales, low-stock inventory warnings, and time-sensitive product drop alerts.
- **Success / Positive (`#10B981`)**: Pure Emerald. Reserved for verified checkouts, available stock counters, and positive order fulfillment states.
- **Surfaces & Grounds**:
  - `bg-base`: `#FAFAFC` provides a warm, ultra-clean studio backdrop that prevents eye fatigue.
  - `bg-surface`: `#FFFFFF` elevates product tiles, modal cards, and sticky navigation headers.
  - `bg-subtle`: `#F1F1F6` houses inactive filters, disabled chips, and horizontal dividers.
- **Typography & Ink**:
  - `ink-primary`: `#09090B` (Deep Carbon) for razor-sharp product naming and high-hierarchy editorial headlines.
  - `ink-secondary`: `#52525B` for specifications, category metadata, and price crossed-out states.
  - `ink-muted`: `#A1A1AA` for placeholder copy and auxiliary microcopy.

## Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans delivers clean geometric rhythm, expressive curves, and pristine rendering across ultra-dense administrative tables and oversized editorial lookbooks.

- **Headlines**: Tight negative tracking (`-0.015em` to `-0.03em`) creates a confident, high-fashion cadence in display sizes.
- **Numbers & Pricing**: Tabular lining is mandatory for all price tags, SKU references, and administrative inventories to align checkout and data views uniformly.
- **Badges & Labels**: Small uppercase or semi-bold titles (`label-sm`, `label-md`) use widened letter spacing (`+0.02em` to `+0.04em`) to ensure legibility on fluorescent surfaces and tiny product chips.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model runs on a flexible 12-column grid system bounded at a maximum container width of `80rem` (1280px) for standard e-commerce views, with an unrestricted fluid breakout for promotional hero lookbooks.

- **Desktop (`>= 1024px`)**: 12 columns with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters and `2.5rem` (40px) outer margins. Product matrices render at 4 or 3 items per row. Admin dashboards utilize an asymmetric `260px` fixed side rail with a fluid main container.
- **Tablet (`768px - 1023px`)**: 8 columns with `1.25rem` (20px) gutters and `2rem` (32px) margins. Product matrices shift into 2 or 3 columns.
- **Mobile (`< 768px`)**: 4 columns with `1rem` (16px) gutters and `1.25rem` (20px) margins. Product grids default to 2 columns with ultra-snug vertical vertical rhythm, allowing high product density above the fold. Category pickers (Damas, Caballeros, Niños) snap into a horizontally scrolling edge-to-edge pill rail.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through translucent surfaces, high-diffusion colored ambient shadows, and crisp ultra-fine outlines rather than dark skeuomorphic drops.

- **Level 0 (Flat Ground)**: Base background `#FAFAFC` with zero elevation.
- **Level 1 (Cards, Product Tiles, Inputs)**: Crisp boundary using a 1px border of `rgba(9, 9, 11, 0.06)` combined with a soft, diffused shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(9, 9, 11, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover States, Floating Selectors)**: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(9, 9, 11, 0.08)`. For primary interactive elements, inject a vibrant color tint:
  - Primary Hover: `0 10px 28px -2px rgba(255, 0, 122, 0.35)`.
  - Secondary/AI Trigger Hover: `0 10px 28px -2px rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.32)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Slide-overs, Floating AI Widgets)**: Multi-stop elevation: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(9, 9, 11, 0.16)`, framed by a 1px edge of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)` on glassmorphic backdrops (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`).
- **Surface Elevation in Admin**: Administrative modules swap colored glows for clean structural layering: pure `#FFFFFF` tiles resting on `#F4F4F8` grounds, using low-opacity dark borders (`rgba(0,0,0,0.08)`).

## Shapes

The system implements a refined Level 2 roundedness model, favoring continuous squircle-like transitions that project modern lifestyle warmth:

- **Buttons & Small Controls**: `0.75rem` (12px) to `1rem` (16px), retaining structural punch while eliminating razor points.
- **Product Tiles & Elevated Panels**: `1rem` (16px) to `1.5rem` (24px) for cards, housing lifestyle imagery with clean corner framing.
- **Badges, Tags & Quick Selectors**: Full pill geometry (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) for size choices (XS, S, M, L), category selectors, and stock badges.
- **Dialogs & Sheet Drawers**: `1.5rem` (24px) on top drawer boundaries and desktop floating modals.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA ("Añadir al Carrito", "Comprar Ahora")**: Filled with `#FF007A`, crisp white text, `rounded-xl`, `font-weight: 700`. Hover applies a subtle scale (`1.02`), brightness shift, and the signature Magenta ambient halo (`0 10px 28px -2px rgba(255, 0, 122, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary CTA**: Translucent Cobalt fill (`rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.08)`), `#4F46E5` label, shifting to solid `#4F46E5` with white ink on hover.
- **Ghost / Tertiary**: Transparent base, `#09090B` text, soft `#F1F1F6` hover fill.

### Category Switcher (Damas, Caballeros, Niños)
- Segmented control on desktop with an animated background slider; mobile features an edge-to-edge kinetic horizontal pill track.
- Active pill displays high-contrast inverted styling (Deep Carbon or Magenta indicator with crisp white text and matching glow).

### Inventory & Stock Badges
- **In Stock**: Pill shape, translucent Emerald ground (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.1)`), solid `#10B981` text, paired with a pulsing 6px green indicator dot.
- **Low Stock ("¡Últimas 2 unidades!")**: Translucent Neon Tangerine (`rgba(255, 107, 0, 0.1)`), `#FF6B00` text, highlighting urgency.
- **Out of Stock**: Inactive grey tone (`#F1F1F6`), `#71717A` label.

### Product Cards
- Aspect ratio 3:4 for fashion photography. 
- Overlaid quick-action wishlist heart button (frosted glass circle with soft blur). 
- Hover triggers smooth scale-up (`scale(1.03)`) with the secondary lifestyle angle revealing effortlessly.
- Bottom details: bold brand category tag, bold price display (`price-prominent`), and color swatch preview pills.

### Floating AI Fashion Stylist / Support Agent
- Positioned floating at the bottom-right corner: 60px circular or rounded-2xl capsule.
- Background: Electric Indigo-to-Magenta angled gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, #4F46E5 0%, #FF007A 100%)`).
- Floating elevation with rhythmic, low-frequency pulse glow (`rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.4)`).
- Expands into an elevated glassmorphic conversational sheet (`backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`), facilitating natural size recommendations and outfit bundling.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px border in `#E4E4E7`. 
- Focus state instantly activates a 1px border in `#4F46E5` plus a 3px ring of `rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.15)`.
- Omnichannel search input features an integrated barcode / image search icon button and trending tags dropdown.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Box size `20px` with `6px` corner rounding (`rounded-md`). Checked state renders solid `#FF007A` with a crisp white check vector.
- Radio buttons feature a concentric ring animation with an elastic pop transition.
